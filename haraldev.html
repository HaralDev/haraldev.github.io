Project Overview

Under the name HaralDev, I took on long-term freelance assignments for several SMEs and start-ups while completing my MSc in Aerospace Engineering at TU Delft. The work spanned two main areas: designing and deploying cloud-based backend systems, and performing feasibility studies and technical research for early-stage companies.

Clients included Startupz, a Dutch startup studio, and Codekeeper, a software escrow and resilience SaaS company. Assignments ranged from building and integrating REST APIs and backend services to conducting market and technology feasibility analyses that informed product decisions. Working part-time across multiple engagements sharpened my ability to deliver independently under tight timelines while balancing full-time academic commitments.
