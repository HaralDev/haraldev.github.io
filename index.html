About Me

My career spans the full lifecycle of space missions — from early concurrent design and systems engineering through hardware integration, environmental testing, launch, and operations. I've worked in Japan, Germany, the Netherlands, and Saudi Arabia, contributing to missions ranging from CubeSats to deep-space rovers.

2025 – present

Director, Spacecraft Integration & Testing

Saudi Space Agency

Leading the Spacecraft Integration & Testing directorate at SSA. Responsible for end-to-end satellite AIT, verification, and mission readiness across agency programmes. Managing a multidisciplinary team and building long-term in-country engineering capability aligned with Saudi Vision 2030.

2023 – 2025

Team Lead, Satellite Engineering

Saudi Space Agency
