About Me

I'm Harald Luiks, Director of Spacecraft Integration & Testing at the Saudi Space Agency (SSA), where I lead a multidisciplinary team of engineers across systems engineering, AIT/AIV, mechanical, electrical, and mission design disciplines.

My career spans the full lifecycle of space missions — from early concurrent design and systems engineering through hardware integration, environmental testing, launch, and operations. I've worked in Japan, Germany, the Netherlands, and Saudi Arabia, contributing to missions ranging from CubeSats to deep-space rovers.

Highlights:
🛰 12 satellites in orbit
🚀 6 space propulsion projects managed
🤖 MMX rover mission — thermal testing of the first rover to land on Phobos
🌍 Space Debris Conference 2024 — Panel Moderator & Scientific Committee (ITU/UNOOSA)

I'm also a strong believer in open knowledge. I maintain a curated GitHub repository of space systems engineering resources — consolidating tools, references, and methodologies that are often scattered or hard to find, to help both new and experienced engineers do better work faster.
